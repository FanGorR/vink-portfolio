Overview

An ongoing creative collaboration with Malaysian Indian influencer and content creator Sasi Savage — handling both cinematography and editing across a range of content formats, from cinematic spec advertisements to sponsored brand recaps.

Each project required adapting quickly to different visual languages: a comedy-to-cinematic bromance edit, a sleek commercial aesthetic for the Gatsby perfume spec ad, a charismatic presenter-led format for Sasi's personal branding video, and a high-energy event recap for the Shopee-sponsored F1 Sepang experience. Four videos, four distinct styles — all shot and cut in-house.
