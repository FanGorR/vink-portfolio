Overview

During my internship at BBK Network, I edited and motion-designed 17 short-form news videos for the company's official social media channels. Each piece was produced end-to-end — from raw footage assembly through motion graphics and colour grade — and all were approved for publication.

Topics spanned Malaysian football's golden age and the match-fixing scandal that unravelled it, civic rights in a multicultural society, F&B economics behind banana leaf rice restaurants, gambling policy and tax revenue, and workplace culture. The series demonstrates a consistent editorial voice adapted across a wide range of subject matter.
